Overview

Decouple the dynamics from the view

We propose DELPHI, a model, and ADYTON, a benchmark, for predicting 4D deformable-object interaction dynamics from a single monocular RGB-D video. Deformable dynamics are non-Markovian in observation space: a frame exposes a front-facing surface, but occluded back faces and interiors carry the state that governs the next instant — momentum in flight, stored elastic energy, plastic history.

Tracking observed points is a Lagrangian commitment: the representation is defined only on the entities you already see, so the state you need leaves it the moment a face rotates out of view or a contact buries it.

To learn complete dynamics regardless of observation completeness, decouple the particle representation from the vision-conditioned dynamics.

Concretely, DELPHI is an operator: from a visual context \(\mathcal{C}\) it produces a whole Eulerian displacement field over all of \(\mathbb{R}^3\), which is then queried at any coordinate — visible surface, occluded interior, or ambient off-object space — without re-tracking anything.

\[ \mathcal{G}_\theta:\; \mathcal{C}\;\longrightarrow\;\bigl(\mathbb{R}^3\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^3\bigr), \qquad u(x)=\mathcal{G}_\theta(\mathcal{C})(x)\;\;\text{for any}\;\; x\in\mathbb{R}^3. \]

The diagnostic that settles it: amodal prediction outperforms visible-only — filling the interior helps the model rather than hurting it.  [[FILL: one-sentence headline result, e.g. amodal interior MDE vs strongest Lagrangian baseline]]

The Benchmark · ADYTON

Ground truth where you cannot look

ADYTON is a Genesis-MPM simulation benchmark of 12,200 dynamic scenes, each a monocular RGB-D video from a static camera paired with dense material-point displacements sampled uniformly through the entire object volume — visible surface, occluded surface, and interior alike. It spans 18 objects across 9 categories, elastic and elastoplastic, driven through 10 motion patterns in three families. Camera, lighting, floor texture, and initial motion are randomized every scene, and the held-out split probes OOD generalization to novel object combinations, interactions, and layouts.

Method · DELPHI

How the field is built

DELPHI reads a monocular RGB-D video and outputs an implicit displacement field over the entire 3D volume, covering visible surfaces, occluded surfaces and object interiors. The field is parameterised by the video's visual tokens, and any 3D point retrieves its motion by cross-attending to them. All positions and motions are expressed in the current camera's frame.

01 · tokens

Video as visual tokens

DELPHI adopts Any4D pretrained on diverse 4D reconstruction tasks, extracts per-frame features that are concatenated over the history. These tokens carry geometric, motion and temporal cues, and they parameterise the output field.

02 · query

Pointwise queries

A lightweight MLP encodes any 3D position \(x\) into a query token. Each point is queried independently, which decouples inputs from outputs and makes the field inference highly parallel.

03 · field

Cross-attention decoding

The query cross-attends to all visual tokens, and an MLP decodes the result into a displacement: \(\mathcal{G}_\theta(\mathbf{o}_k)(x)=\mathrm{MLP}(\mathrm{CrossAttn}(q,F_k))\).

Relative spatial biasEach token sits at the centroid of its back-projected patch. The offset \(x-p^i_s\) passes through Fourier features \(\gamma\) and an MLP \(\phi\) and is added to the attention logit, \(\operatorname{softmax}\big(q^{\top}f^i_s/\sqrt{d}+\phi(\gamma(x-p^i_s))\big)\). Because the bias sees both distance and direction, it learns motion-specific priors, such as looking below a falling object or ahead of a sliding one.
Multi-scale tokensA purely local bias makes the model nearsighted about the far future. Average-pooling fine tokens into coarse ones, each located at the centroid of the union of its pixels, gives fine-to-coarse context that the query attends to with the same spatial bias.
LoRA adaptationAny4D was pretrained on real-world data, so LoRA adapts the backbone to our synthetic scenes. DELPHI is trained with an L2 loss on displacements.

Nomenclature: Drawn from the sacred topography where Zeus's eagles marked the center of the earth to divine unseen futures, Delphi was revered as the navel of the world and the seat of prophetic counsel. It mirrors our ambition towards the World Model, acting as an anywhere-queryable continuum that answers queries at any coordinate in space. Adyton, the oracle's inviolate inner sanctuary, embodies our benchmark, holding the elusive physical truths that nature withholds yet our model strives to decipher.
